Privacy Policy for BookMyChair

Last Updated: January 17, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to BookMyChair. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how our application handles information when you use our service.

2. Information We Collect

BookMyChair is designed with privacy in mind. The app allows hairdressers to create their profiles and manage appointment schedules directly on their devices. We collect minimal information:

  • Hairdresser Information: Name, business name, contact phone number, and working hours that you voluntarily provide when setting up your profile.
  • Schedule Data: Appointment times and availability settings that you create for managing bookings.

3. No Customer Data Collection

BookMyChair does not require customer login or registration. We do not collect, store, or process any personal information from customers making appointments.

4. Data Storage

All data you enter into BookMyChair is stored locally on your device only. We do not:

  • Upload your data to remote servers
  • Store your information in cloud databases
  • Transmit your data to third parties
  • Create backups on external systems

You have complete control over your data, and it remains exclusively on your device.

5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

Since BookMyChair stores all data locally on your device and does not transmit data to our servers, we do not share, sell, rent, or trade your information with any third parties.

6. Data Security

The security of your data depends on the security measures you have in place on your device. We recommend:

  • Using device passcodes or biometric authentication
  • Keeping your device's operating system updated
  • Being cautious about device access permissions

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data remains on your device until you choose to delete it. You can:

  • Delete individual appointments or schedule entries within the app
  • Remove your entire profile and all associated data
  • Uninstall the app to permanently remove all data from your device

8. Children's Privacy

BookMyChair is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control and can:

  • Access your data at any time through the app
  • Modify or update your information
  • Delete any or all data
  • Export your schedule information if needed

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new privacy policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

11. Analytics and Tracking

BookMyChair does not use analytics services, tracking tools, or advertising technologies. We do not monitor your usage of the app.
