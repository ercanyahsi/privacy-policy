Privacy Policy

Number Beats
Last updated: December 30, 2025

Your privacy is our priority. Number Beats is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

Simple Summary: Number Beats does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

1. Information We Collect

We collect ZERO personal information.

Number Beats does not collect, store, transmit, or share any of the following:

  • Personal identification information (names, email addresses, phone numbers)
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or gameplay statistics
  • Cookies or tracking technologies
  • Photos, contacts, or other device data

2. How the App Works

Number Beats operates entirely offline on your device:

  • No User Accounts: The app does not require registration, login, or account creation
  • Local Storage Only: All game progress and settings are stored locally on your device
  • No Internet Connection Required: The app functions completely without network access
  • No External Servers: No data is transmitted to or from any external servers

3. Third-Party Services

Number Beats does not integrate with any third-party services, including:

  • Analytics platforms (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase)
  • Advertising networks
  • Social media platforms
  • Cloud storage services
  • Payment processors

4. Children's Privacy

Number Beats is designed to be safe for children of all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

In compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), our app:

  • Does not collect personal information from children
  • Does not enable children to publicly post or distribute personal information
  • Does not contain in-app purchases or advertisements
  • Does not include links to external websites or social media

5. Data Security

Since no data is collected or transmitted, there are no security risks associated with data breaches or unauthorized access to personal information. All game data remains securely stored on your local device and is never shared.

6. Your Rights

As we do not collect any personal data, there is no information to:

  • Access or review
  • Update or correct
  • Delete or erase
  • Export or transfer

You maintain complete control over your device and can uninstall the app at any time, which will remove all locally stored game data.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal and regulatory reasons. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

Continued use of Number Beats after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

8. Legal Compliance

This Privacy Policy complies with:

  • Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)
  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)
  • Other applicable data protection laws
